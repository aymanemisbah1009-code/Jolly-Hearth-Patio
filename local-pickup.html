Local Pickup Guide

Pick up your Jolly Hearth & Patio order with confidence

Eligible coffee machines, espresso makers, and barista accessories can be collected from our location in Northport, Alabama. This page covers what to expect, what to bring, and how collection works once your order is ready.

How local pickup works

Place your order first

Choose your item online and complete checkout before heading to our pickup location.

Wait for pickup confirmation

We will contact you as soon as your order is packed, verified, and ready to be collected.

Bring your order details

Have your order confirmation, a valid ID, and any collection message ready when you arrive.

Before you arrive

  • Make sure you have received a pickup-ready confirmation from our team.
  • Bring a valid photo ID and your order number.
  • If someone else is collecting for you, contact us in advance so we can note it on the order.
  • For high-value items, we may ask for an extra confirmation step before release.

Need help first?

If you are unsure whether a product is available for local pickup, please contact us before placing the order so we can confirm availability and timing.
